How it works

No spreadsheets, no guessing from one lucky sale. Every morning, a robot does the boring part.

Scan eBay

We search eBay US for every toy and card in each Happy Meal series and collect every Buy It Now listing.

Throw out the noise

Lots, full sets, graded slabs, fakes and listings for other years are filtered out, leaving single, ungraded items.

One fair price

We take the median of what's left, so one silly $500 listing can't move the number, and save it to build a daily price history.

What the numbers mean

Toy Ticker borrows ideas from stock sites. Here's the cheat sheet.

Median askThe middle asking price of today's matching listings. Half ask more, half ask less.
Lowest askThe cheapest listing today: roughly what you'd pay to buy one right now.
Listings ("volume")How many copies are for sale. More listings usually means prices are about to soften.
ReturnsHow much the price moved over 1 day, 1 week, 1 month and so on. "–" means not enough history yet.
Full setThe total of every item in a series at today's median ask: what a complete collection is worth.
ConfidenceHow much to trust a price. High means lots of listings that mostly agree; Low means only a few, so one listing can move it.
Market mapEvery item as a block: bigger means more valuable, greener or redder means a bigger daily move.

Heads up: these are asking prices, not completed sales. Sellers usually ask a bit more than buyers end up paying, so treat the median as a ceiling and check eBay's sold listings before you price something.

What we track

The current Happy Meal plus the series before it. New series are added when they hit restaurants.

Questions

Why asking prices and not sold prices?

eBay only shares completed-sale data with a small number of approved partners. Asking prices are public, update daily and move in the same direction as sales, so they're a reliable signal of where the market is going.

How often do prices update?

Once a day, early morning US time. Each update adds one point to every item's chart and returns.

Why is my toy missing a price or a photo?

If nobody has listed a single, ungraded copy yet, there's nothing fair to measure. Photos come from eBay listings, so they appear once a good listing shows up.

Where is "My collection" saved?

Only in your browser. There are no accounts and no tracking, and nothing about your collection is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data clears it.

Is this investment advice?

No. Happy Meal toys are made by the million and prices can swing on a single listing. Collect what you love; use Toy Ticker to avoid over- or under-paying.
